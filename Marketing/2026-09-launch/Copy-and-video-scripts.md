# Ready-to-review copy and demo scripts

Draft only. Use the released app for captures; the new onboarding/macros work must be published before advertising it as available.

## Short captions

**Simple tracking**
Food simple. Tracking simple. Cave Cals keeps your daily calories easy to see, with quick logging and optional macros. Start with a target, or just start tracking.

**Voice**
Say what you ate. Review the estimate. Get on with your day. Voice logging in Cave Cals helps turn everyday meals into a food log you can edit.

**Quick Add**
Same breakfast? Less typing. Cave Cals learns from your food history so familiar foods are ready to log again.

**Weigh-ins**
One day at a time. Log a weigh-in, revisit a missed day, and see your history alongside your food-tracking routine.

**Founder voice**
I wanted a calorie tracker that was easier to stick with. So I built Cave Cals: simple daily totals, quick food logging, and a little caveman personality. I'd love to hear what makes tracking feel easy—or frustrating—for you.

## Video A: “Breakfast, again” (18–22 seconds)

| Time | Visual | Voice / on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Real breakfast on a table; hand-drawn caveman corner graphic | “Same breakfast. Why type it again?” |
| 3–9s | Actual Quick Add: add eggs, then toast with blue plus buttons | “Tap. Tap. Breakfast logged.” |
| 9–14s | Show the updated calorie container; briefly open an editor | “Your daily total. Easy to adjust.” |
| 14–20s | Return to the clean home view and brand end card | “Cave Cals. Tracking simple.” |

Use a staged test diary, not a real person's health information. Do not speed up the capture in a way that disguises actual interaction delays. If sped up for pacing, label it.

## Video B: “Say lunch” (20–25 seconds)

| Time | Visual | Voice / on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Person holding phone; voice ad illustration cut-in | “Lunch already happened.” |
| 3–8s | Tap Speak Food, record a normal meal description | “I had a turkey sandwich and an apple.” |
| 8–15s | Real processing transition and Review Scan | “Review the estimate.” |
| 15–20s | Edit one portion if needed, then Add All | “Make it yours. Then log it.” |
| 20–25s | App + brand end card | “Cave Cals. Say it. Log it.” |

Use actual results from the app; don't claim perfect recognition. The app offers an introductory allowance with eligibility limits, not unlimited free AI. Avoid “free forever” or an unconditional “10 free scans” headline.

## Video C: “A plan without a marathon” (18–25 seconds)

## Video D: restaurant search (15–20 seconds)

Open search, type a restaurant with verified provider results, tap a title to review serving/calories, then use the blue plus to log. Hook: “Lunch out. Still easy to log.” Keep fatsecret attribution where the app displays it. Show the actual selected serving; restaurants and nutrition listings can change.
