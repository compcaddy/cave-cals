Product Hunt permits self-submission. Its guide calls for a direct product URL, a product-only name, a tagline up to 60 characters, a description up to 500, and at least two gallery images. It recommends a square thumbnail and 1270 × 760 gallery images. Use genuine product screens, and ask for feedback rather than votes. [Product Hunt launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch)

The r/iosapps moderator update lists 25 community karma for main-feed promotion, a problem/comparison/cost structure, and special rules for AI-assisted development and AI-centric products. Its App Shelf thread is the alternative for developers who do not qualify. Cave Cals needs an honest description of both its development and optional AI features; don't assume it qualifies for a standalone post. Recheck current rules and ask moderators if uncertain. [Moderator update](https://www.reddit.com/r/iosapps/comments/1t00stp/riosapps_moderation_update_improving_post_quality/)

Do not treat weight-loss support groups as ad inventory. No mass posting, unsolicited direct messages, automated community participation, or purchased votes is part of this plan.

## After sharing

Record the source, exact message, date, and any available campaign-link metrics in experiment-log.csv. Keep feedback about confusing controls separate from download counts. A small sample is useful for fixing obvious friction; it does not establish conversion lift or long-term retention.
