## Description

You eat. App track. Keep going.

Cave Cals is a simple food diary with a little prehistoric personality. Log what you ate, see your daily total, and get on with your day. No account signup required.

START YOUR WAY
A short optional setup estimates a daily calorie target from your details, activity, goal, and preferred pace. Review and adjust it before starting. You can also enter your own goal or track without one. Estimates are for supported adult inputs; a manual route is available when the calculator does not fit your needs.

EVERYDAY LOGGING
• Search restaurant and branded foods, scan a barcode, or enter calories yourself.
• Add familiar foods quickly with Quick Add. Pin your favorites and save meals.
• Edit portions, serving sizes, and calories before logging.
• See optional protein, net carbs, and fat, with optional daily macro goals.
• Log weigh-ins, move between days, and see your weight history.
• Open logging tools from iPhone Home Screen widgets.
• Keep your diary on your iPhone, with private iCloud sync when available.

OPTIONAL PHOTO AND VOICE ESTIMATES
Meal Scan turns a food photo into editable food and nutrition estimates. Voice Log lets you describe a meal, review the result, and log it. Cave Cals+ also supports importing a meal from a public website link.

Try up to 10 combined Meal Scan and Voice Log analyses without a subscription, until you have logged 100 regular food entries. After either limit, Cave Cals+ is required to continue scanning. Paid plans include up to 30 photo/voice analyses per day and 300 per calendar month. Manual logging, food search, barcode lookup, saved meals, and weight tracking remain free.

Choose a monthly or yearly subscription. Local prices, billing periods, and any available introductory offer are shown before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in your Apple Account settings at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

YOUR DETAILS, YOUR CHOICE
Plan answers and weigh-ins stay in protected local storage on this device. Weight history does not sync through the Cave Cals iCloud diary. Optional Apple Health sharing writes your weigh-ins only with permission. You can forget saved plan details while keeping your calorie goal and weigh-ins.

AI estimates can miss portion sizes and ingredients. Review and correct them before saving. Selected media and requested AI inputs are sent to OpenAI through our backend; your full diary and calorie-plan answers are not sent for analysis. Starting targets are estimates, not medical advice or a promise of weight loss.

## Reviewer notes addition

The new setup is optional and has no login or purchase requirement. Build my plan asks for age, gender/reference values, height, current weight, activity, and goal/pace. It calculates an editable estimate locally. Set my own goal and Just start tracking remain available from the welcome screen. Unsupported or clinician-led cases get a manual route without an automated target. Existing users can open You -> Build/Update calorie plan; they are not forced through setup. Forget plan details removes saved answers without deleting the calorie goal or weigh-ins. Onboarding adds no camera, microphone, tracking, or Health authorization prompt.
