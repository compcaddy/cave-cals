# Calories in every fast-food breakfast (ranked)

13 slides: hook, #10 → #1 (biggest last), a bonus "lightest pick", and the Cave Cals ending.
Re-render after editing copy or numbers: `python3 ../../render_breakfast_ranked.py` from `assets/social`.

- `9x16/` → TikTok (1080 × 1920, fills the screen)
- `4x5/` → Instagram (1080 × 1350, the tallest feed size)

## How to post

**TikTok (photo mode):** + → Upload → pick the 13 images in order from `9x16/` (they're numbered) → choose a trending sound (something low-key; the text carries it) → paste the caption → Post. Swiping is the point: the ranking makes people swipe to see #1.

**Instagram (carousel):** + → Post → tap the multi-select icon → pick the 13 images in order from `4x5/` → keep the 4:5 crop → add music if you like → paste the caption → Share. (Instagram allows up to 20 images.)

AirDrop the folder to your iPhone first; both apps post best from the phone.
