# Zog and Zena: character catalog

Cave Cals' mascots for marketing, ads and videos. **Read this before making any Cave Cals marketing material**, and use these images rather than generating new ones unless a pose is missing.

All poses are transparent PNGs (about 330–930 px wide). Previews on a dark background: `preview-on-dark.png`, `preview-app-and-together.png`, `preview-before-after.png`. Source sheets (cream background) are in `../` (`*-sheet.png`, `zog-reference.png`). Made in ChatGPT (chat "Create character sheet" in Phil's account), cut with `../cut_poses.py`.

## Style
Clean cartoon, thick black outlines, flat colors with light crayon texture, tan skin, burnt-orange pelt (#b84718) with brown spots. Background when not transparent: cream #fbf8f2. Brand font: Schoolbell (`../Schoolbell-Regular.ttf`).

- **Zog** (cave-man): big messy black hair and full beard, wide friendly smile, one-shoulder pelt. Reference: `../zog-reference.png`.
- **Zena** (cave-woman): **keep her happy eyes with the small eyelash flick** (Phil's favorite detail); no beard; rosy cheeks; messy bun with a bone; one-shoulder pelt.
- Voice: short caveman lines ("Me eat. App count."). See `../../../11-Brand-Voice-Copy-Bank.md`.

### `before-after/`
| File | Shows | Where it can be used |
|---|---|---|
| zog-habit-before / zena-habit-before | frustrated, buried in stone tablets | **ads and organic** |
| zog-habit-after / zena-habit-after | happily photographing food (drumstick / salad) with scan brackets | **ads and organic** |
| zog-body-before / zena-body-before | rounder body, pelt covering the belly, relaxed, no phone | **organic posts only, never ads** |
| zog-body-after / zena-body-after | slimmer, both arms up cheering "yay", no phone | **organic posts only, never ads** |

**Rule:** Meta, TikTok and Google ban before-and-after body images in weight-loss ads (cartoons included), and the playbook guardrails say no before/after bodies in ads. Use the **habit** pair in any paid ad or App Store asset. Body pairs are for organic posts only, kept kind (no shame), and TikTok may still limit their reach.
