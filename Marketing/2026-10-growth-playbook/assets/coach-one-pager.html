How it works

  1. Client installs Cave Cals and redeems your code (2 min)
  2. Set goals together: calories, protein, carbs, fat
  3. They log: tap usuals, snap, say, or scan
  4. Every Sunday: Progress → print icon → Share the Weekly Recap to you
  5. You review weekly averages, not daily noise
