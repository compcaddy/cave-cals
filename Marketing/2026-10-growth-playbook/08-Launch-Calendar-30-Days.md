# Launch calendar: day by day for 30 days, then week by week to New Year

**L = the day the new version is live on the App Store** (not TestFlight). Everything before L is prep. Post times are for US audiences: 7–9am, 12–1pm, or 7–10pm local.

---

## Before launch (L−7 to L−1)

- [ ] Claim @cavecals on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, Threads, Pinterest, and Reddit (profile photo: the caveman launch art; bio from [11](11-Brand-Voice-Copy-Bank.md#bios))
- [ ] Enroll in the **App Store Small Business Program** if you haven't (15% instead of 30%)
- [ ] Create App Store **campaign links** for each account and channel ([12](12-Metrics-and-Budget.md#campaign-links))
- [ ] Create **Offer Codes**: `CAVETRAINER`, `ZOGLAUNCH`, and one per creator test
- [ ] New screenshots uploaded with the release ([06](06-App-Store-Optimization.md)); update the promotional text
- [ ] Film the **content bank**: 10 faceless demos, 5 slideshows, 3 Zog skits, and the first trainer shoot (12 videos). **Launch with 30 videos in the bank.**
- [ ] Line up 10 friends/family/gym people who'll download on L and leave an honest rating if they like it (no incentives; they just need to know it's live)
- [ ] Draft the Product Hunt page ([09](09-Launch-PR-Community.md))
- [ ] Gym flyer printed ([assets/gym-flyer.pdf](assets/gym-flyer.pdf)); trainer code created
- [ ] Rating prompt shipped in this build? If not, it goes to the top of the next build.

## Launch-day posts (copy-paste ready)

**Personal Instagram / Facebook:**
> I built an app. 🍖 Cave Cals is a calorie tracker for people who hate calorie tracking. It learns your usual foods, you can snap a photo or just say what you ate, and there's a caveman. Free on iPhone. I'd love for you to try it and tell me what's confusing. Link in bio.

**X / Threads launch thread:**
> 1/ Just shipped a big Cave Cals update: a calorie tracker simple enough for a caveman. 🍖
> 2/ Quick Add learns your usual foods from your history (time of day, weekday habits, what you eat together). Breakfast is one tap.
> 3/ Snap a plate or say what you ate. You always review the estimate before it's logged.
> 4/ Free: food search, barcode scanning, manual logging, meals, weigh-ins, weekly progress. Paid only for photo/voice AI.
> 5/ No account. Diary stays on your iPhone with private iCloud sync.
> 6/ Built solo. Would love brutal feedback: [App Store link]

**LinkedIn:**
> After [X] months of nights and weekends, the new Cave Cals is live. The problem: most people quit calorie tracking within two weeks because it feels like data entry. The approach: learn what people actually eat and make the next log one tap, with photo and voice when typing is too much. And give it a personality (a caveman) so it's something people *want* to open. If you know a personal trainer or dietitian who'd give honest feedback, I'd be grateful for an intro.
