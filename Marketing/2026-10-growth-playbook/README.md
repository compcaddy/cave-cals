# Cave Cals growth playbook: start here

Prepared September 28, 2026 for the release after 1.0.4. It builds on [the September launch packet](../2026-09-launch/README.md), which was a cautious first test plan. This one is about growth and revenue: what Cal AI and other winning apps did, adapted to a solo developer with a personal trainer on board.

**Nothing has been posted, sent, purchased, or changed in App Store Connect or the app.** Everything here is a draft for you to use.

**Phone-friendly version:** [Cave Cals Growth Playbook page](https://claude.ai/artifact/48AK17TmEesQHhUr57a1Z1). It's private: top moves checklist, revenue calculator, hook bank with copy buttons, scripts, templates, and asset previews. Share it with your trainer from the page's Share menu.

## The 10 moves that matter most (in order)

| # | Move | Why | Cost | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Replace the App Store screenshots.** The live ones show the old blue theme. | Every channel lands on this page; a mismatched look loses installs | $0 | [06](06-App-Store-Optimization.md), [new screenshots](assets/app-store-screenshots/store/) |
| 2 | **Add a rating prompt** (the app has none) | Ratings drive search rank and page conversion | 1 small app change | [02 §1](02-Paywall-and-Onboarding.md#priority-1-ask-for-ratings-cheap-big) |
| 3 | **Put a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan, and test $39.99/yr** | Worth about +65% revenue at the same traffic (see model) | App Store Connect + RevenueCat, no code | [02 §2](02-Paywall-and-Onboarding.md#priority-2-put-a-trial-on-the-yearly-plan-and-show-it-at-the-plan-reveal), [12](12-Metrics-and-Budget.md#what-the-money-could-look-like-planning-scenarios-not-forecasts) |
| 4 | **Show a skippable paywall at the end of onboarding** ("Try for $0.00" + trial timeline) | Cal AI's single biggest lever; manual logging stays free | App change (**owner decision**: reverses "no paywall in setup") | [02 §2](02-Paywall-and-Onboarding.md) |
| 5 | **Start 3 TikTok/IG accounts and post 100 videos in month one** | Cal AI's $0 engine: faceless demos, slideshows, a character | Time | [03](03-Video-Scripts.md) (50 scripts, 70 hooks) |
| 6 | **Trainer filming day, every other week** (12 videos in 2 hours) | Trust transfer, like MacroFactor + Jeff Nippard at gym scale | Free subscriptions + revenue share | [05](05-Trainer-Partnership.md) |
| 7 | **Offer Codes per trainer/creator** (`CAVETRAINER` = 1 free month) | Attribution of *paying* users; gives people a reason to share | $0, App Store Connect | [02 §4](02-Paywall-and-Onboarding.md#priority-4-referral-codes-and-offer-codes-the-trainer-channel-depends-on-this) |
| 8 | **Test 10 micro creators (~$500), retain the winners** | Cal AI's main channel, at indie scale | ~$500 | [04](04-Creator-Program.md) |
| 9 | **Gym flyer + 30-Day Cave Challenge** | Local, free, high-trust installs, and great content | Printing | [flyer](assets/gym-flyer.pdf), [challenge sheet](assets/cave-challenge-sheet.pdf) |
| 10 | **Plan for January now** (Cave Wrapped, New Year event, creator bookings) | The biggest install week of the year for diet apps | – | [01 §6](01-Growth-Strategy.md#6-the-seasonal-calendar-the-big-one-is-january), [08](08-Launch-Calendar-30-Days.md), [10](10-Retention-and-Viral-Loops.md) |

### Known gaps in the assets
- **No current-theme captures of a Review Scan result, Barcode Scan, Siri, or the widget.** Those need a real AI result, a camera, or the Home Screen, so film them on an iPhone (scripts in [03](03-Video-Scripts.md)).
- **The Weekly Recap capture shows the current week empty** because it was captured on a Monday. Its six-week table is full. Re-capture later in a week for a fuller top half.
- The captures come from a temporary UI test that was run and then removed (`UITests/ProgressUITests.swift` was restored byte-for-byte). To re-capture, follow the instructions in [MarketingCaptureTests.swift.txt](assets/app-store-screenshots/MarketingCaptureTests.swift.txt).

## Your weekly rhythm (about 6 hours a week)

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Monday, 30 min | Fill in the [weekly scorecard](12-Metrics-and-Budget.md#weekly-scorecard). Pick one thing to double down on and one to stop. |
| Monday, 90 min | Batch-film 10 faceless demos and slideshows |
| Every other Wednesday, 2 hr | Trainer shoot |
| Thursday, 60 min | Zog skits |
| Friday, 30 min | Creator review ([04](04-Creator-Program.md#operations)) |
| Daily, 20 min | Post, reply to comments, one video reply |
| Monthly | Paywall experiment check (RevenueCat), trainer feedback session, promo text rotation |

## Guardrails (short version)
No guaranteed weight loss. No before/after bodies in ads. 18+ targeting. Disclose paid partnerships, including the trainer's. Never send health data to ad platforms. Never pay or reward anyone for ratings. Be kind about bodies. Details are in [01 §7](01-Growth-Strategy.md#7-guardrails-these-protect-the-business-not-just-ethics).
