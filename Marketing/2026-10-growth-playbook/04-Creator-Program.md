# Creator program: Cal AI's playbook at a $500/month scale

Cal AI's main growth channel was paying creators a **flat monthly retainer** for about 4 posts a month, ~250 creators at peak. Their key rules:

1. **Micro creators beat big names** on return per dollar. Real, niche audiences.
2. **Test once, then lock in.** A creator whose test post converts gets a long-term deal right away, before a competitor does.
3. **Flat fees, not pure commission.** Competing apps offer guaranteed pay, so commission-only offers get ignored. Add performance bonuses on top.
4. **Judge by math:** cost per 1,000 views (CPM) against revenue per 1,000 views (RPM). They targeted RPM ≥ 2× CPM.
5. **Native content, not ads.** Creators film the app the way they'd show a friend.

Sources: [FunnelFox](https://blog.funnelfox.com/cal-ai-influencer-marketing/), [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/cal-ais-marketing-strategies-lessons-from-a-400k-mrr-success-story).

## The Cave Cals version

### Phase 0: before paying anyone (week 1–2)

- Ship the release and make sure the paywall and trial are working (see [02](02-Paywall-and-Onboarding.md)). **Paying for traffic into a leaky funnel is the #1 way indie apps burn money.**
- Create App Store **Offer Codes** per creator (`CAVE[NAME]`: 1 free month) and **campaign links** per creator.
- Post 30+ of your own videos first so you know what a good Cave Cals video looks like. The brief shows creators your best one.

### Phase 1: test 10 creators for about $500 (weeks 3–6)

| Tier | Followers | Test fee | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nano | 2k–10k | $25–60 | 1 video + 30 days of usage rights for Spark Ads |
| Micro | 10k–50k | $75–200 | 1 video + Spark code |
| Mid | 50k–250k | $250–600 | Only after you've got 2+ winners |

Pick 8 nano and 2 micro to start. Budget: ~$500.

**Who to look for** (search TikTok/IG for these, filter to US, 18+ audience):

- "What I eat in a day" creators who aren't diet-extreme
- Meal-prep / budget high-protein cooking
- Gym-goers documenting a cut ("day 34 of my cut")
- Busy-mom fitness
- Nurses / teachers / shift workers who post food content (relatable and trusted)
- Personal trainers with small followings (they also recruit clients, a double win)
- **Avoid:** extreme low-calorie content, "what I eat on 800 calories," under-18 creators or audiences, body-checking content, medical claims

**Signals that predict conversions** (more than follower count):

- Median views per video ≥ 20% of followers (engaged audience)
- Comments ask "what app is that?" or "what's your macros?"
- They already show apps or food logs on camera
- Their audience is 60%+ US (ask for a screenshot of audience analytics)

### Phase 2: retain winners (weeks 6–12)

After each test post has run 7 days, score it:

- **Installs** via campaign link, plus offer-code redemptions
- **Views**
- **RPM** = (attributed 12-month revenue estimate ÷ views) × 1,000
- **CPM paid** = (fee ÷ views) × 1,000

| Result | Action |
| --- | --- |
| RPM ≥ 2× CPM | **Offer a retainer now:** 4 videos/month at their test rate × 3.5 (a small bulk discount), 3-month term, performance bonus |
| RPM between 1× and 2× CPM | One more test with a different angle (the script library has 50) |
| RPM < 1× CPM, but content was great | Buy usage rights and run it as a Spark Ad instead |
| Flop | Thank them and move on. Don't argue about results. |

**Retainer structure that works for small budgets:**

- Base: e.g. $200/month for 4 videos (nano/micro)
- Bonus: **$1 per 1,000 views over 20k** on any video in the first 30 days, capped at $300/month
- Bonus: **$5 per offer-code redemption** that becomes a paid subscriber (verified in RevenueCat)
- Spark Ads rights for the retained videos during the term plus 30 days

### Phase 3: scale (once 3+ creators have been profitable 2 months in a row)

- Cal AI got to 150+ retainers by mid-2024 with a small in-house team. At that point, hire a **part-time creator manager** paid a base plus a bonus tied to creator ROI. Their whole job is sourcing 20 creators a week, sending briefs, tracking results, and paying on time.
- Use a creator marketplace or tool (TikTok Creator Marketplace, Collabstr, Insense, Billo for pure UGC) for sourcing volume.
- Build a **"Cave Crew" ambassador program** for users who love the app: free Cave Cals+ plus a bonus for each paid referral via their code.

## The one-page creator brief

**Cave Cals: creator brief**

**What it is:** A calorie tracker for people who hate calorie tracking. iPhone only. Free to download, with free search, barcode scanning, and manual logging. Cave Cals+ adds photo and voice logging (10 free tries).

**The feeling:** Lazy, fun, relieved. "Oh, that was easy." Not "transform your body."

**Pick ONE thing to show** (your choice):
1. **Quick Add:** it learns your usual foods. Log breakfast with one tap.
2. **Snap it:** photo of your plate → calories and macros → review → add.
3. **Say it:** tell it what you ate → it logs it.
4. **Type it:** "pizza 300" → logged.
5. **Free barcode scanning.**
6. **Done eating:** tap it at night → "Day done. Cave closed."

**Must do**
- Show the real app on your real phone within the first 3 seconds
- Say it in your own words and give your honest opinion
- Tap the paid-partnership toggle, or put **#ad** in the first line of the caption
- Mention "free on the App Store" and your code **[CODE]** for a free month of Cave Cals+
- Link in bio: [campaign link]

**Please don't**
- Promise weight loss or specific results ("I lost 10 lb with this app")
- Show before/after bodies, pinch skin, or say foods are "bad"
- Show very low calorie totals as a goal
- Say the AI is "100% accurate." It's an estimate you review. That's part of the pitch.
- Mention competitors by name negatively

**Hooks that have worked** (use or ignore):
- "I quit every calorie app until this one."
- "This app learned my breakfast."
- "Me eat. App count." (Bonus points for caveman voice.)
- "Why am I paying to scan barcodes?"

**Deliverables:** 1 video, 15–45s, vertical. Send the draft before posting (we only check facts, not style). Post within 7 days. Share a Spark code / partnership ad code after posting.

**Payment:** $[fee] via PayPal/Venmo business within 3 days of posting.

## Contract checklist (a simple one-page agreement is fine)

- Deliverables, posting deadline, platform(s)
- Fee, bonus terms, payment timing
- **Disclosure required** (FTC Endorsement Guides): platform toggle and/or #ad
- Usage rights: organic reposting on Cave Cals accounts plus paid ads for [30/90] days
- Draft review for factual accuracy only
- No weight-loss guarantees or medical claims
- Creator is 18+ and the audience is primarily adult
- Either side can end a retainer with 14 days' notice
- Store signed contracts outside this repository (they contain names, addresses, and payment details)

## Operations

Track every creator in [assets/creator-pipeline.csv](assets/creator-pipeline.csv): handle, platform, followers, median views, contact date, status, fee, code, campaign link, post date, views at day 7, installs, redemptions, estimated revenue, CPM, RPM, decision.

**Weekly creator review (30 minutes, Fridays):**
1. Which posts crossed 7 days? Score them.
2. Any retainer offers to make?
3. Top-performing hook this week → add to the brief, and film your own version.
4. Top-performing video → request Spark code, boost $20/day for 3 days as a test.
5. Source 10 new creators.
