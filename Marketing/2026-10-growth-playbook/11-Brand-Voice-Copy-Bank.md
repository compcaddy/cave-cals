# Brand voice and copy bank

## The caveman voice rules

Zog (the caveman) is **proud, food-obsessed, confused by modern things, and secretly great at math.** He's never mean about bodies. He cheers for consistency, not smallness.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Short words, present tense: "Me eat. App count." | Long sentences in caveman grammar (hard to read) |
| Drop articles: "Log breakfast?" | Mock anyone's body, weight, or eating |
| Refer to self as "Zog" or "me" | Use caveman voice for prices, legal, errors, or accessibility labels (plain English there) |
| Fire, rock, mammoth, cave, club, hunt | Stone-age stereotypes about groups of real people |
| Celebrate: "Cave strong!" | Guilt: "You failed" / "Bad day" |
| "Close enough is fine" | "Perfect tracking or nothing" |

**Readability check:** a line should make sense to someone skimming at a glance. If you have to decode it, rewrite it.
