# Short-form video library: 50 ready-to-film scripts

For TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels. The same file works everywhere: film vertical 9:16 at 1080×1920, and keep text inside the middle 80% so captions and buttons don't cover it.

**Feature accuracy:** every script shows a real feature in the new build. Film with a staged demo diary, not anyone's real health data, and never speed up the app to fake speed. If you cut for pacing, that's fine. Just don't claim "3 seconds" over a 12-second action.

## How to run this (the Cal AI engine at indie scale)

### Accounts (start with 3, grow to 5)

| Account | Face | Format | Posts/day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **@cavecals** (brand) | Zog the caveman + app | Skits, memes, demos, replies to comments | 1–2 |
| **@[trainer]** (their own account, or a new "[Trainer] × Cave Cals" one) | Your trainer | Gym tips, myth-busting, client-style stories, protein | 1 |
| **@cavecals.recipes** or **@lazycalories** (faceless) | Hands + screen | Slideshows, "calories in X," screen demos | 1–3 |
| Later: **@phil builds** | You | Build-in-public, founder story | 3/week |
| Later: second faceless | – | Pure slideshow utility (restaurant guides) | 2 |

TikTok ranks each video on its own, not by followers. **Volume matters more than polish for the first 60 days.** Aim for 100 videos across accounts in month one, then double down on the formats that get saves and profile clicks.

### Rules for every video

1. **Hook in the first second.** On-screen text *and* spoken. No logo intro. Start mid-action.
2. **Show the app within 3 seconds** in demo videos. The magic moment is the product.
3. **One idea per video.** 12–25 seconds is the sweet spot. Long enough for a loop, short enough to finish.
4. **End on a loop or a question**, not "download now." Question endings drive comments, which drive reach.
5. **CTA lives in the caption and the pinned comment:** "Cave Cals on the App Store (free)." Put the link in bio. Use App Store campaign links per account (see [12-Metrics](12-Metrics-and-Budget.md)).
6. **Reply to comments with video.** Cal AI and Duolingo both got huge reach from video replies ("@user asked if it knows pho. Let's see.").
7. **Paid? Disclose.** Trainer or creator posts in exchange for money or a free subscription get the platform's paid-partnership toggle or #ad.
8. **Body-neutral.** No pinching fat, no "disgusting," no scale-shame. Energy is "easy and fun," not "fix yourself."

### Filming kit (under $60)

Phone + $20 clip-on mic + $25 ring light or a window. Screen recordings: iPhone Control Center → Screen Recording, with Display Zoom off and Do Not Disturb on. For the caveman: a $25 faux-fur tunic, a foam club, and a messy wig. Commit to the bit.

## Series A: Your trainer (12 scripts)

The trainer is the MacroFactor/Jeff Nippard play at gym scale: trust transfers from them to the app. Film all 12 in one 2-hour session (see [05-Trainer-Partnership](05-Trainer-Partnership.md)). Keep them in their gym clothes, in the gym, talking like they do to clients. **Their words, not a script read.** Use the beats as prompts.

### A1. "The #1 reason my clients don't lose weight"
- **Hook (0–2s):** Trainer mid-set, racks weight, turns to camera: "Number one reason my clients don't lose weight? They don't know what they eat."
- **Beat (2–10s):** "Not because they're lazy. Because tracking apps are a chore. So they quit by week two."
- **Beat (10–18s):** Phone in hand, over-shoulder shot: taps a Quick Add + for breakfast, the calorie count rolls up. "This one learns what you eat. Breakfast, one tap."
- **End (18–22s):** "If you won't track, you're guessing. Stop guessing." Loop back to racking the weight.
- **Caption:** Tracking doesn't have to be a second job. What's your biggest struggle with logging food? 👇 #personaltrainer #caloriedeficit #weightlosstips
- **Pinned comment:** App's called Cave Cals, free on iPhone.

### A2. "How much protein do you actually need?"
- **Hook:** "How much protein do you actually need? Way simpler than TikTok says."
- **Beats:** Give their real rule of thumb (commonly ~0.7–1 g per pound of goal body weight for lifters; **let the trainer state their own guidance**). "Most of my clients are at half that."
- **Demo:** Home screen, protein column showing grams vs goal. "I have clients set a protein goal and just watch this one bar."
- **End:** "Hit protein, the rest gets easier."
- **Caption:** Save this for your next grocery run. #protein #fitnesstips #highprotein

### A3. "Weekend eating is why Monday's weigh-in is up"
- **Hook:** Trainer holding a donut box: "This is why your Monday weigh-in is up."
- **Beats:** "It's not the donut. It's the three untracked meals around it." Shows the Progress page's week chart with a tall Saturday bar.
- **End:** "Track the weekend. Not to feel bad. To know."
- **Caption:** Weekend = 2/7 of your week. #weightlossjourney

### A4. "Your weight went up overnight? Don't panic."
- **Hook:** Stepping off a scale, fake-panicking: "Up 2 pounds overnight?!"
- **Beats:** "Water, salt, carbs, sleep. One weigh-in means nothing. The weekly *average* is what I watch." Shows the Weekly Recap card: average weight + change vs last week.
- **End:** "Look at weeks, not days."
- **Caption:** Your trainer's scale rules. Share with someone who panics every morning. #scalevictory #weightloss

### A5. "What I tell clients who hate tracking"
- **Hook:** "You hate tracking calories? Me too. Here's the deal I make with clients."
- **Beats:** "Track for 3 weeks. Not forever. Just long enough to learn what your usual meals are." Shows Quick Add rows filling in: "After a week the app already knows your usual foods."
- **End:** "Three weeks. Then decide."
- **Caption:** The 3-week tracking challenge. Who's in? 🙋

### A6. "I made my client track for 7 days. Here's what we found."
- **Hook:** Holding a printed **Weekly Recap PDF**: "My client tracked for 7 days. Look at this."
- **Beats:** (Use a staged demo recap, or a real client with **written consent**.) "Averaged 2,600 on weekends, 1,700 on weekdays. Protein was under 80 grams every day." Point at the PDF's weekly chart.
- **End:** "You can't fix what you can't see."
- **Caption:** Coaches: this weekly recap prints on one page. Clients just tap print. #onlinecoach
- **Note:** This is the "coach-ready" pillar. It also recruits other trainers.

### A7. "3 foods sabotaging your protein goal"
- **Hook:** "Three 'protein' foods that are mostly not protein."
- **Beats:** Holds each: a protein bar (check label), peanut butter (fat), a "protein" granola. Log one with a barcode scan in the app to show the real protein. "Barcode scanning, free, by the way."
- **End:** "Read the label, or just scan it."

### A8. "Stop doing cardio to burn off dinner"
- **Hook:** On a treadmill: "Stop doing cardio to 'burn off' dinner."
- **Beats:** "40 minutes here is about one slice of pizza. You can't outrun your fork. Know the number, then decide."
- **Demo:** Types `pizza 300` in search → one-tap add row. "That's the log. Done."
- **End:** "Train to get strong. Track to get lean."
- **Safety note:** Keep it about awareness, not punishment. No "earn your food."

### A9. "Log food without touching your phone"
- **Hook:** Trainer, hands full with dumbbells: "Hey Siri, log food with Cave Cals."
- **Beats:** Siri asks "What did you eat?" Trainer: "Chicken wrap and a protein shake." Siri reads back what was logged and today's total.
- **End:** "Hands full. Log done."
- **Note:** Siri logging of unfamiliar foods uses one AI scan; foods logged before are free. Film with a food you've logged before or with scans available.

### A10. "Eating out? Here's how I'd log it."
- **Hook:** At a restaurant table: "Eating out doesn't have to wreck your week."
- **Beats:** Snap the plate with Meal Scan → Review Scan list with the calories → tweak a portion with the pencil → Add All. "Estimate, adjust, done. Close enough beats not at all."
- **End:** "Enjoy the meal."

### A11. "The weigh-in routine I give every client"
- **Hook:** "Here's the weigh-in routine I give every single client."
- **Beats:** "Same time, after the bathroom, before food. Log it. Ignore it. Check the weekly average on Sunday." Shows logging today's weight in About You, then the Progress weight chart.
- **End:** "Boring works."

### A12. "Trainer reacts: calorie apps"
- **Hook:** Green-screen over MyFitnessPal/Cal AI/Cave Cals App Store pages: "Trainer ranks calorie apps."
- **Beats:** Fair, honest pros and cons. Cave Cals: "free barcode, learns your meals, has a caveman." **Be factual about competitors; only say what you've checked on the day.**
- **End:** "Best app is the one you'll actually use."
- **Disclosure:** The trainer must disclose they partner with Cave Cals.

## Series B: Zog the caveman (10 skits)

A person in a caveman costume (you, the trainer, a friend) who speaks in caveman talk. Duolingo's owl works because the character's personality never changes: **Zog is proud, food-obsessed, confused by modern things, and secretly very good at math.** Film on a phone; low budget is the joke.

### B1. "Cave man discover calorie"
Zog holds a mammoth leg (turkey leg). Grunts. Pulls out a phone and scans it with Meal Scan. Squints at the number. **On-screen:** "Mammoth leg: 1,100 cals." Zog slowly puts half back. **Text:** "Cave man learn portion control." CTA caption: "Me eat. App count. 🍖"

### B2. "Zog vs. barcode"
Zog sniffs a granola bar and tries to bite the barcode. Friend hands him the phone; Zog taps the barcode icon, scans, and the product appears. Zog, amazed: "Stripes... have... numbers?!" **Text:** "Barcode scanning. Free. Like fire."

### B3. "Zog tries other calorie apps"
Zog opens a (blurred, unnamed) cluttered app. Ads, popups, "Premium required." Zog smashes a rock (off screen) and switches to Cave Cals: Quick Add, tap, done. "ZOG HAPPY." Keep competitors unrecognizable.

### B4. "POV: the caveman in your calorie app at 11pm"
You open the fridge at 11pm. Zog appears in the fridge-light glow: "You... done eating today. Zog saw you tap button." Show the **Done eating for today** card, then "Day done. Cave closed." Zog slowly closes the fridge door.

### B5. "Zog learns about macros"
Friend: "You need to track your macros." Zog: "Macro... big rock?" Cut to Zog pointing at the protein/carbs/fat icons on the Home card: "Drumstick. Wheat. Drop. ZOG UNDERSTAND." (The macro icons really are a drumstick, a wheat stalk, and a drop.)

### B6. "Zog says it"
Zog too lazy to type, lying on a rock: "PHONE. ZOG ATE. TWO EGG. ONE TOAST. BIG COFFEE." Voice Log turns it into a Review Scan list. Zog, smug: "Zog not type. Zog TALK."

### B7. "Cave man intermittent fasting"
Zog chasing a mammoth all day, no food. Sunset. **Text:** "Cave man invented intermittent fasting by accident." Then Zog eats everything. "Then invented Cave Cals to count it."

### B8. "Zog weekly recap"
Zog holding the printed Weekly Recap like a stone tablet: "ZOG AVERAGE DOWN ONE POUND." Victory dance. **Text:** "Weekly averages. Not daily panic."

### B9. "Zog at Thanksgiving"
Zog at a family table, logging with voice: "Turkey. Stuffing. Three pie." App logs it. Grandma: "Are you counting calories at Thanksgiving?!" Zog: "Zog count. Zog still eat pie." **Text:** "Log the feast. Enjoy the feast."

### B10. "Zog meets the personal trainer" (crossover)
Trainer tries to teach Zog a squat. Zog squats perfectly (cavemen squatted all day). Trainer: "OK... what did you eat today?" Zog shows Cave Cals with protein at goal. Trainer, stunned. **Text:** "Cave man already ahead."

## Series D: Slideshows / carousels (12)

TikTok Photo Mode and Instagram carousels. Slideshows earn **saves**, one of the strongest ranking signals. Make them in Canva or with the templates in `assets/`. Slide 1 = hook; last slide = soft CTA. Use real nutrition numbers from labels or official restaurant nutrition pages and **double-check every number**; comments will.

**D1. "High-protein snacks under 200 calories"**
1. High-protein snacks under 200 cals (save this) · 2. Greek yogurt cup: ~15–18 g protein, ~100–150 cal · 3. String cheese ×2 · 4. Beef jerky 1 oz · 5. Cottage cheese ½ cup · 6. Hard-boiled eggs ×2 · 7. Tuna pouch · 8. Edamame 1 cup · 9. "I log these with one tap in Cave Cals (they're my Quick Adds)."

**D2. "Calories in every fast-food breakfast, ranked"**: one slide per chain. Verify against official nutrition pages. Last slide: "Search any restaurant in Cave Cals."

**D3. "Things I stopped doing that made calorie tracking easy"**
1. Things I stopped doing that made tracking stick · 2. Weighing every gram · 3. Logging at night from memory · 4. Using apps with 40 buttons · 5. Starting over every Monday · 6. Panicking at daily weigh-ins · 7. Paying to scan barcodes · 8. What I do now: log as I eat, use Quick Add, check the weekly average.

**D4. "Same meal, different calories"**: homemade vs restaurant burrito bowl, latte vs cold brew, and so on. Last: "Log either in seconds."

**D5. "POV: you finally found a calorie app you don't hate"**: meme slides with app screenshots (Home, Quick Add, Done eating, caveman art).

**D6. "Protein per dollar at the grocery store"**: chicken thighs, eggs, canned tuna, Greek yogurt, lentils, whey. Use local prices and state the date.

**D7. "What 1,800 calories looks like (3 ways)"**: three real days, photographed. Last slide: logged day in the app.

**D8. "Halloween candy: fun-size calories"**: one slide per candy from the label.

**D9. "Starbucks orders under 200 calories"**: verify on Starbucks' nutrition page.

**D10. "Signs you're under-eating protein"**: have the trainer approve the content.

**D11. "My calorie app has a caveman and here's his best lines"**: screenshots of copy like "Day done. Cave closed." and "You Eat. App Track. Weight Drop."

**D12. "5 ways to log food without typing"**: Quick Add, Meal Scan, Voice Log, Siri, barcode.
