Resubmission following the previous App Review feedback:

1. Mainland China availability: We removed Mainland China from the app's distribution territories. App Store Connect now shows Mainland China as unavailable.

2. Subscription-screen presentation (Guideline 2.1): We changed the paywall presentation to use navigation within the existing screen instead of nested sheets, and added a guard against duplicate dismissal requests. These changes address the subscription page disappearing immediately after it was opened, including when the iPhone app runs on iPad in compatibility mode.

3. Updated screenshots and branding: The App Store screenshots have been replaced with current app captures. The optional premium subscription is branded Cave Cals+.

Review instructions:
- No app account or login is required. Complete the optional calorie-goal setup or skip setting a goal.
- Open Settings and select the upgrade option to open the Cave Cals+ subscription page. Close/cancel returns to the prior screen without making a purchase.
- Restore purchases is available in Settings and on the paywall. Apple may ask the reviewer to authenticate with the App Store account during purchase or restoration.
- Manual calorie logging, food search, barcode lookup, Quick Add, saved meals, history, goals, and widgets are free. Cave Cals+ provides meal-photo analysis, voice analysis, and recipe/link import.
- To review Meal Scan, tap the drumstick scan button, take or select a food photo, then tap Scan and Analyze. To review voice logging, tap the microphone. A subscription is required for analysis; users can review and edit estimates before adding them.
- Purchases use Apple's in-app purchase flow. The production backend supports verified Apple sandbox transactions. Use a physical iPhone or iPad for device attestation and sandbox purchase testing.

The changes above address the reported availability and paywall-presentation issues. No demo account credentials are needed.
