No account signup or demo login is required. Manual logging, food search, barcode lookup, saved meals, calorie plans, macros, weight tracking, and the standard Progress charts are free.

CLANS AND OPTIONAL ACCOUNT (NEW IN 1.2.0)
Tap the campfire icon in Home's header > Sign in with Apple (Google is also offered). Signing in is optional; the rest of the app works without it. Sign-in requires agreeing to the terms (no objectionable content). Create a clan, then share its invite link or code; joining needs an admin's approval by default. A second account is needed to see another member, nudges/cheers, and boards. A clan of two is free; letting in a third member requires an admin with Cave Cals+.
User-generated content is limited to display names, optional profile photos, and clan names (names are filtered). There is no free-text chat; nudges and cheers are preset messages. Tap a member > Report name or photo, or Block; clan names from the clan's Clan settings > Report clan name. Reports reach us and are reviewed within 24 hours. Members can join anonymously as "Cave person".
Account deletion: Me (person icon) > your account row > Delete account.
Clans receive only a small daily summary per member (logged, full day, % of goal, done eating); clans never receive food, calories, or Health data. Weight-loss challenges send only percentages from weigh-ins logged in Cave Cals and are limited to adults.

APPLE HEALTH (HEALTHKIT)
Settings (gear icon on Home) > Apple Health, marked with the Apple Health icon: Calories & macros and Weigh-ins switches. Also in Me (person icon) > Apple Health. Off by default. Cave Cals writes calories, macros, and weigh-ins. New in 1.2.0: with Weigh-ins on, it also reads body mass (weight only) so readings from a smart scale appear as weigh-ins; Settings > Apple Health > Sync now. Health data is never sent to our backend. Screen recording on a physical iPhone: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/GuFt72SOAkg

FIRST LAUNCH
Setup is optional: choose Build Plan or Just start tracking. Plans support loss, maintenance, or gain. The post-setup subscription offer can be closed without purchasing.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Cave Cals+ is sold only through the regular monthly and yearly In-App Purchase subscriptions, with Apple introductory offers for eligible new subscribers; the paywall shows the introductory price, its length, and the renewal price. There is no in-app promo code entry. Restore purchases is on the paywall and in the subscription area.

AI FEATURES
Meal Scan and Voice Log (Cave Cals+ after an introductory allowance of up to 10 analyses), Cave Coach (Progress > On-Target vs. Over Days), and recipe import send only what the user selects, or a summary of logging patterns for Cave Coach, through our backend to OpenAI. New in 1.2.0: while Track macros is on, the name and calories of foods missing macros are sent to estimate them automatically; this is free and does not use scans. Progress Photos are never uploaded.

AI AND DEVICE REQUIREMENTS
AI requests and Clans sign-in need internet and a real signed device for Apple's App Attest. Paid access is server-verified. Please use the normal purchase flow for subscription review.

ICLOUD
Diary, meal-type settings, and Progress preferences use private iCloud sync. Weight history and Progress Photos stay local. Privacy policy: https://cavecals.com/privacy
