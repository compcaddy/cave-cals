No account signup or demo login is required. Manual logging, food search, barcode lookup, saved meals, calorie plans, macros, weight tracking, and the standard Progress charts are free. This is the normal Cave Cals app, not our separate development app.

FIRST LAUNCH
Setup is optional: choose Build Plan or Just start tracking. Plans support loss, maintenance, or gain. Targets and AI results are estimates, not medical advice. The post-setup subscription offer can be closed without purchasing.

CAVE COACH
Open Progress (chart icon), scroll to On-Target vs. Over Days, then Cave Coach > Get coach tips. Cave Cals+ is required. It returns three suggestions; Get new tips replaces them (up to three requests per day). The selected range ends yesterday. Meaningful comparisons require at least three on-target and three over-goal days; historical food can be logged by changing Home's date. The request sends a summary of logging patterns, including selected food names, calorie/macro averages and timing, through our backend to OpenAI. It does not send the full diary or read Apple Health. Tips are labeled AI suggestions, not medical advice.

PROGRESS PHOTOS
Open Progress > Progress Photos > Take Photo or From Library. One photo per day is free; additional angles that day require Cave Cals+. The camera offers angle selection, a ghost of the previous photo, front/back cameras, and a 10-second timer. View all photos > Compare supports side-by-side and slider views. These progress photos stay on the device and in its backups; they are never uploaded to our backend, OpenAI, or CloudKit. They are separate from Meal Scan photos chosen for AI analysis.

OTHER NEW FLOWS
Settings > Meal types enables optional meal grouping. Tap Protein, Carbs, or Fat on Home to edit a day's values or estimate missing values with AI. Meals > New Meal > Import a recipe accepts a link, photos, or clipboard content; recipe import requires Cave Cals+. Voice Log also offers Type instead for a written food description. Food estimates can be reviewed and corrected before saving.

AI AND DEVICE REQUIREMENTS
AI requests need internet and a real signed device for Apple's App Attest. Meal Scan and Voice Log share an introductory allowance of up to 10 successful analyses until 100 regular food entries; paid access is server-verified. Developer settings can appear in TestFlight/sandbox for internal testing, but are hidden in App Store builds. Please use the normal purchase flow for subscription review.

HEALTH AND ICLOUD
Apple Health sharing is optional and off by default. The app only writes calories, macros, and weigh-ins; it never reads Health data. Diary, meal-type settings, and Progress preferences use private iCloud sync. Weight history and Progress Photos stay local. Privacy policy: https://cavecals.com/privacy
